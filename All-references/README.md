# Centralized Research References

**Trust-Aware Agentic AI for Autonomous Cyber Incident Response and Resilient Recovery**

This collection is maintained in the repository's root-level `All-References/` directory. It consolidates the external sources used to motivate, design, and evaluate the project.

## Research themes

1. Agentic AI and autonomous incident response.
2. Ransomware mitigation and resilient recovery.
3. Incident-response playbooks and operational practice.
4. Cyber-threat intelligence and structured threat information.
5. Trust assessment and multi-source evidence fusion.
6. Recovery safety, service dependencies, and post-recovery validation.

## How to use this collection

1. Prefer the publisher, standards body, or original author page as the canonical source.
2. Record the version, publication date, DOI, and access date when available.
3. Read the full paper before making claims about its methods, results, or limitations.
4. Keep a clear distinction between a paper's stated limitations and gaps inferred by this project team.
5. Cite the original source in reports and presentations; do not cite this folder as the source of the research.
6. Recheck links and bibliographic metadata before final submission.

## Copyright and repository policy

- Include PDFs only when the publisher or author makes them openly available for redistribution, or the applicable licence permits repository hosting.
- For paywalled papers, retain the citation and publisher URL; do not upload a copy obtained through institutional access unless the licence or permission allows redistribution.
- For news articles, retain metadata, a short original summary, and the article URL. Do not republish full article text.
- Do not commit downloaded HTML error pages with a `.pdf` extension.
- Keep any licence or permission statement alongside a redistributed document.

## Status and verification note

This documentation is a structured working draft prepared from the references identified in the project review material and the accompanying source list. Before treating any result, author list, venue, DOI, or limitation as final, verify it against the full paper or official publisher record. In particular, distinguish:
- publication year from preprint posting year;
- conference/journal version from an earlier preprint;
- reported experimental results from general claims;
- project-inferred research gaps from limitations explicitly acknowledged by authors.
