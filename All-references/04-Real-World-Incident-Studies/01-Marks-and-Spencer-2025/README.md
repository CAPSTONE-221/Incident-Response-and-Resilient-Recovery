# Marks & Spencer Cyber Incident (2025)

## Purpose of this case study
This folder records the Marks & Spencer (M&S) cyber incident as a real-world motivation and recovery case study. The sources below are secondary reporting and should be used with careful attribution.

## Relevance to the project
The incident is useful for discussing:
- business-service disruption caused by a cyber incident;
- the operational consequences of prolonged service unavailability;
- recovery sequencing and restoration of customer-facing services;
- the financial and reputational consequences of an incident;
- why recovery completion should include validation of service availability and security, not merely restoration of files.

## Analysis cautions
- Do not infer technical details that the cited articles do not establish.
- Attribute reported attribution to the source; do not present attribution as independently proven by this project.
- Verify financial figures against the original reporting and distinguish direct company costs from wider economic estimates.
- Do not copy full article text into this repository.
